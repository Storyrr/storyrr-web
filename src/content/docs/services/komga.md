[Komga](https://komga.org/) tells Storyrr which eBooks, manga and comics you already have.

## Connect

In **Settings → Komga**:

- **Address:** for example `http://komga:25600`.
- **API key:** in Komga, **Account settings → API keys**. Use an administrator's key if you want to import users.

Press **Test** and **Save**, then choose the **libraries** that count and press **Sync libraries**.

## Matching

Komga series carry no shared identifier: titles are matched by series title (and its alternate titles), as "probably owned".

## Sign-in and users

Komga accounts can sign in to Storyrr (with their email and password) and be imported, like [Audiobookshelf's](/services/audiobookshelf/#sign-in-and-users).
