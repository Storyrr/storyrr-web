Discover is the home page: rows of what's trending, the latest requests, what just became available, genres and upcoming books.

## Arranging rows

In **Settings → General → Discover**, administrators turn rows on or off and move them up or down. **Reset to default** brings back the original layout. Everyone sees the same Discover.

## Adding rows

**Add a row** shows Books or Audiobooks with a sort and filters, under a title you choose. The filters are those of the Books and Audiobooks pages:

- **Books** (Hardcover): genres, edition language, release years, pages, minimum rating;
- **Audiobooks** (Audible): genre, author, narrator, publisher;
- both: hide what's already in the library or requested, and only languages Chaptarr accepts.

For example, a **Manga in French** row: Books, the Manga genre, French editions, sorted by most read. Its **See all** opens the Books page with the same filters.
