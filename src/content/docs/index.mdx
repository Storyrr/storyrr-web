Movies and TV have Seerr. Storyrr is the same idea for the book side of a homelab: one place where the people you invite find a book, request it, and follow it until it shows up in their reader.

## What it does

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Search and discover" icon="magnifier">
    Audiobooks from Audible's catalog; books, manga and comics from Hardcover. Series, authors,
    genres, readers' ratings, and a Discover page you arrange yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Requests with approval" icon="approve-check">
    Per-user permissions and request limits, auto-approval, a blocklist, and the target Chaptarr
    folder chosen per request: Books, Manga or Comics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Knows what you have" icon="open-book">
    Checks your Audiobookshelf, Kavita, Komga and Jellyfin libraries and Chaptarr's imports, so
    titles you own show as available. Users report a wrong edition or language as an issue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sign in with your media server" icon="seti:lock">
    Invite-only accounts, or sign in with Audiobookshelf, Kavita, Komga or Jellyfin, and import
    their users in a click.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notifications" icon="comment">
    Email, Discord, Slack, Telegram, ntfy, Gotify, Pushover, Pushbullet, webhooks and Web Push,
    shared or personal, in each user's language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made to be shared" icon="laptop">
    English, French, German and Spanish. Installable as an app on phones. Works on your local
    network and through a tunnel at the same time.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it fits in

<Steps>

1. **A user requests a title.** They find it in Storyrr, with its status: available, requested, or a Request button.
2. **You approve it** (or it's approved automatically), choosing the Chaptarr folder and quality profile if you want.
3. **Chaptarr fetches it**, monitoring only the requested book in the requested format.
4. **Storyrr sees it arrive**, through Chaptarr's webhook and your media servers, and notifies the user that it's available.

</Steps>

:::caution[Pre-release]
The v1 features work, but expect changes until 1.0.
:::
